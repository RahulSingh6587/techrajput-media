Subtitle Studio - Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Subtitle Studio is a private desktop application run by its owner on their own computer. It has no servers and no other users.

Google data it accesses

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own channel and read basic channel and video details.
  • Blogger (blogger): to create and update posts on the owner's own blog.

Every upload or post happens only after the owner explicitly confirms it.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Subtitle Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
